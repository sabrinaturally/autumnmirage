When life asks a lot of you

Parent, professional, caregiver, friend: each role comes with its own demands, and they rarely take turns. When it all piles up, overwhelm sets in and your emotions can feel hard to balance. A session gives you a safe space to pause, put words to what you’re feeling and find a more balanced self again, with gentle support along the way.

Self-awareness first, remedies second

Dr Edward Bach developed his flower remedies in the 1930s as a gentle, complementary practice for emotional wellbeing. I use them as a prompt: the conversation about what you are feeling, and why, is as valuable as the bottle you take home. My approach is rooted in self-care, curiosity and zero judgement.

I’m Sabrina Tan, a Bach Foundation Registered Practitioner registered with the Bach Centre. You can check this by searching my name in the Bach Centre’s practitioner directory, or visit their website for more details about the remedies and Dr Bach’s work.

What happens in a session

  1. We talkYou describe what has been weighing on you, in your own words.
  2. We chooseTogether we pick the remedies that match the feelings you named.
  3. Your remedy reaches youI prepare your customised blend and explain how to use it: take 4 drops, 4 times a day, either directly or in a drink. Remedies can be couriered to you within 3 days. If your request is urgent, please say so when you email me so arrangements can be made.

Bach flower remedies are a complementary practice and are not a substitute for medical or mental health care. They are not intended to diagnose, treat or cure any condition. If you are unwell, please see a doctor.

The 38 remedies

Dr Bach grouped his 38 remedies into seven themes. Each remedy is linked to a state of mind. This is here for curiosity, not for self-diagnosis. In a consultation, we choose together what fits you.

Tap a remedy to see what it is linked to.

01 · Fear

For feelings associated with fear and apprehension.

02 · Uncertainty

For feelings associated with doubt and indecision.

03 · Present moment

For feelings of drifting away from the here and now.

04 · Loneliness

For feelings of being apart from others.

05 · Sensitivity

For feelings of being easily affected by others and by change.

06 · Low spirits

For feelings of discouragement and despair.

07 · Caring for others

For feelings of over-concern for the wellbeing of others.

What’s in your remedy
In Dr Bach’s tradition, the 38 remedies are made from the energy of wild flowers, trees, plants and natural spring water, carried in water. Your blend is mostly water with the remedies, plus a very small amount of alcohol that acts as a preservative.
